C3 — Campus to Corporate Club

President

March 2026 – Present

C3 (Campus to Corporate Club) is a student-led technical community dedicated to bridging the gap between academic learning and industry expectations. As President, I lead a team of passionate students in organizing technical sessions, hands-on workshops, and collaborative learning activities that help members build practical skills and prepare for their careers.

Our focus is on creating a culture of continuous learning through topics such as software development, networking, cybersecurity, Git & GitHub, AI, cloud technologies, hackathons, and career development. By encouraging peer-to-peer learning and teamwork, C3 empowers students to gain real-world experience, improve problem-solving skills, and grow into industry-ready professionals.

Through C3, I have strengthened my leadership, communication, project management, and mentoring skills while helping fellow students learn, collaborate, and achieve their goals.
